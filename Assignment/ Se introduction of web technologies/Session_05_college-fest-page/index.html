Spandan 2026

Events

Three days of music, coding contests, dance battles, street plays and food stalls bring the whole campus together for the biggest student celebration of the year.

Guests

Alumni founders, local artists and a famous radio jockey will inspire students with short talks, live performances and open question sessions on the main stage.

Contact

Write to the student council at fest@example.edu or visit the coordination desk in the main library lobby between ten in the morning and five in the evening on any working day.
